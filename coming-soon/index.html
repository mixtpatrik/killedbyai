Upcoming AI Shutdowns

Coming soon to the graveyard: the deprecation schedule for 39 AI products with confirmed death dates. Already announced, just counting down.

Gemini Flash & Pro for Free Users

1d left

From October 9, people using the Gemini app on a personal account without a Google AI plan lose the Flash and Pro models and are left with Flash-Lite. AI Plus subscribers keep Flash but lose Pro, on a date Google emails them individually. It is the second free-tier squeeze in five months, after the May 2026 switch to compute-based usage limits.

Killed by: Google Shutdown: October 9, 2026 Replacement: Gemini Flash-Lite; Google AI Plus for Flash, or AI Pro / Ultra for Pro Source

Azure OpenAI GPT-4.1 nano

6d left

GPT-4.1 nano, the smallest of the April 2025 GPT-4.1 family, retires on Azure OpenAI on October 14, 2026, 18 months after launch, with no replacement listed. Its siblings got a reprieve: Microsoft pushed gpt-4.1 and gpt-4.1-mini back to April 14, 2027. Nano's own date has flipped back and forth in Microsoft's docs since August; as of the September 23 update it is October 14 again. Fine-tuned nano deployments keep running until October 14, 2027.

Killed by: Microsoft Azure Shutdown: October 14, 2026 Replacement: None listed Source

GPT-5.5 in ChatGPT & Codex

6d left

OpenAI's April 23 flagship leaves ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work and Codex on every plan on October 14, 174 days after launch. Codex users on paid plans are pointed to GPT-6 Sol and Free and Go users to GPT-6 Luna, both 'when available'. The API keeps gpt-5.5, and Azure lists it until October 26, 2027, so the model outlives its own chat app by a year.

Killed by: OpenAI Shutdown: October 14, 2026 Replacement: GPT-6 Sol (paid plans) / GPT-6 Luna (Free & Go), when available Source

Claude Sonnet 4 on Amazon Bedrock

Anthropic retired claude-sonnet-4-20250514 from its own API on June 15, but Bedrock kept serving it for another 121 days. AWS marked it Legacy on April 14: new customers were locked out, and existing ones could lose access after 15 days of inactivity. On July 14 it moved into 'public extended access', where AWS tells customers to expect higher prices set by the provider. End of life is October 14 in all 23 regions listed.

Killed by: AWS Shutdown: October 14, 2026 Replacement: None named by AWS (Anthropic recommends claude-sonnet-4-6) Source🪦 Related: the Claude Sonnet 4 & Opus 4 tombstone →

Sora 2 on Azure OpenAI / Foundry

7d left

Sora outlives its maker's own shutdown by three weeks: OpenAI switched its Sora API off on September 24, but Microsoft pushed the Azure retirement from September 15 to October 15, with no replacement. Until then, the last place to generate a Sora video is a Microsoft data centre.

Killed by: Microsoft Shutdown: October 15, 2026 Replacement: None listed Source🪦 Related: the OpenAI Sora tombstone →

Gemini 2.5 Pro / Flash / Flash Lite (Vertex AI)

12d left

Google retires the Gemini 2.5 family on Vertex AI (now the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform) on October 20, 2026, four days after the October 16 date it previously gave. That date blocks new projects and any project that hasn't called the model since July 22; per a re-issued September 15 customer notice quoted publicly, existing workloads keep running until January 28, 2027 for Flash-Lite and March 31, 2027 for Flash and Pro (August 6, 2027 in the KR, BR and FR regions). On the Gemini Developer API the models are still 'not deprecated', but since September 18 only projects that used them before can call them.

Killed by: Google Shutdown: October 20, 2026 Replacement: gemini-3.8-flash / gemini-3.5-flash / gemini-3.5-flash-lite / gemini-3.1-flash-lite Source

You can still run them; you just have to host them yourself.

When gemini-omni-1.1-flash went GA on August 27, Google said the preview endpoint would be 'deprecated on September 30, 2026'; the deprecations table sets the actual shutdown for no earlier than October 22, which would make a 114-day life. The three Veo 3.1 previews (veo-3.1-generate-preview, veo-3.1-fast-generate-preview and veo-3.1-lite-generate-preview) go the same day, to the same replacement.

OpenAI GPT-4, GPT-4 Turbo, GPT-3.5 Turbo, o1, o3-mini & o4-mini

15d left

One date kills three eras. On October 23, OpenAI removes gpt-3.5-turbo (the engine that launched ChatGPT), gpt-4, gpt-4-0613, gpt-4-turbo and gpt-4-1106-preview (the models that defined 2023), and most of its first generation of reasoning models — o1, o1-pro, o3-mini, o4-mini — plus gpt-4o-2024-05-13, gpt-4.1-nano and gpt-image-1. o3 and o3-pro follow on December 11. Every fine-tune built on them dies the same day. Seventeen table rows, six months' notice.

Killed by: OpenAI Shutdown: October 23, 2026 Replacement: gpt-5.6-sol / gpt-5.6-terra / gpt-5.6-luna / gpt-image-2.5-sunburst or -flare Source

xAI Grok Imagine Image Quality

25d left

xAI retires the grok-imagine-image-quality slug on November 2, after 60 days' notice. Calls will not fail. They are redirected to grok-imagine-image-2.0 at the 'low' quality setting, $0.01 per image cheaper, and only the model field in the response shows the change. grok-imagine-image-pro was already redirected to the 'quality' model in May and now gets forwarded a second time.

Killed by: xAI Shutdown: November 2, 2026 Replacement: grok-imagine-image-2.0 Source

OpenAI Models in Cursor

35d left

OpenAI is ending its contract to supply models to the AI code editor Cursor on November 12, 2026, using a change-of-ownership clause after SpaceX completed its $60B purchase of Cursor in August. 'It boils down to trust,' said OpenAI's Thibault Sottiaux, citing Elon Musk's history of breaking contracts. Built-in GPT access ends, but developers can still bring their own OpenAI API keys or use OpenAI's IDE extension, and Cursor says OpenAI models were only about 5% of its traffic. Cursor says it is 'speaking with the OpenAI team to resolve this.'

Killed by: OpenAI Shutdown: November 12, 2026 Replacement: Bring-your-own OpenAI API key or OpenAI's IDE extension; Claude and other models in Cursor Source

Reddit RSS Feeds & Public API

36d left

Killed by AI in the literal sense. Reddit turns off RSS on November 13 — 'RSS has now become a common surface for large-scale scraping and automated abuse' — stops new public API requests on October 31 and closes the remaining public endpoints in March 2027. The data now goes to paying AI licensees instead; that revenue line grew 24% last quarter.

Killed by: Reddit Shutdown: November 13, 2026 Replacement: None for outside readers; registered apps only (deadline Jan 12, 2027) Source

Google Opal & Gemini Gems

40d left

Google switches off Opal, its Labs no-code builder for AI mini-apps launched in July 2025, on November 17. The 'Gems made by Labs' it powered go the same day, and neither migrates anywhere. Ordinary Gems, the custom chatbots Google shipped in 2024, are being converted into Gemini Skills: personal accounts from November, Workspace business in March 2027 and education in June 2027. Opal workflow files stay in Google Drive as plain text; they just stop running.

Killed by: Google Shutdown: November 17, 2026 Replacement: Gemini Skills / Google AI Studio Source

Gemini Live & TTS Preview Models

40d left

Five voice previews leave the Gemini API no earlier than November 17. Two are Live API models, gemini-3.1-flash-live-preview and gemini-2.5-flash-native-audio-preview-12-2025. The other three are text-to-speech models: gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview, gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts and gemini-2.5-pro-preview-tts. The 2.5 TTS models spent 18 months in preview and never left it; the 3.1 Live preview, released in March, gets eight. The replacements, gemini-3.8-live and gemini-3.8-flash-tts, went GA on September 15 and 22.

Killed by: Google Shutdown: November 17, 2026 Replacement: gemini-3.8-live / gemini-3.8-flash-tts / gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts Source

Azure OpenAI o-series (o1, o3, o4-mini, o3-pro)

42d left

Microsoft folded its staggered schedule into one date: o1, o1-pro, o3, o3-deep-research, o3-mini, o3-pro and o4-mini all retire November 19, 2026. That pushed o3-mini back from October 1, o4-mini from October 16 and o1, o1-pro and o3 from October 21, and pulled o3-pro forward from December 17. gpt-5.6-sol is the recommended replacement, or gpt-5.6-terra for o3-mini and o4-mini; codex-mini goes four days earlier with none. Azure now keeps o1 and the minis about four weeks longer than OpenAI's own API, and kills o3 and o3-pro about three weeks sooner.

Killed by: Microsoft Azure Shutdown: November 19, 2026 Replacement: gpt-5.6-sol / gpt-5.6-terra (o3-mini, o4-mini) Source

Azure OpenAI GPT-4o (2024-05-13)

62d left

The original May 2024 GPT-4o snapshot retires from Azure on December 9, and Microsoft uses it as the worked example in its lifecycle docs. Global Standard, Data Zone Standard and Standard deployments are force-upgraded to gpt-5.6-sol. Provisioned deployments get no upgrade and must migrate by hand, and deployments set to NoAutoUpgrade simply start returning 410 Gone. It outlives OpenAI's own gpt-4o-2024-05-13, which goes on October 23, by seven weeks. The later 2024-08-06 and 2024-11-20 versions last until April 14, 2027.

Killed by: Microsoft Azure Shutdown: December 9, 2026 Replacement: gpt-5.6-sol (auto-upgrade on Standard deployments; Provisioned must migrate manually) Source

Killed by: OpenAI Shutdown: December 11, 2026 Replacement: Plugins (ChatGPT + Codex); GPT instructions become a skill Source

OpenAI Self-Serve Fine-tuning

90d left

OpenAI is closing self-serve fine-tuning in three steps. Since May 7, 2026, organizations that had never fine-tuned can't start; since July 2, organizations with no fine-tuned inference in the previous 60 days are locked out; on January 6, 2027, active existing customers lose the ability to create new fine-tuning jobs. Existing fine-tunes keep serving until their base model retires, and the six on legacy bases (ft-gpt-3.5-turbo, ft-gpt-4, ft-gpt-4.1-nano, ft-o4-mini, ft-babbage-002, ft-davinci-002) shut down October 23.

Killed by: OpenAI Shutdown: January 6, 2027 Replacement: Prompt caching / system prompts Source

Pipedream Workflows & String

174d left

Pipedream will shut down Workflows, its developer automation platform, and String, its prompt-to-agent builder, on March 31, 2027. All Workflows data, including stored credentials and OAuth tokens, is deleted by April 30. Workflows is already in maintenance mode, with sign-ups closed. Killed by: Pipedream Shutdown: March 31, 2027 Replacement: None (Pipedream Connect continues; JSON export for workflows) Source

Microsoft Foundry Agent Service (classic)

174d left

Microsoft's managed agent service entered preview in December 2024 as Azure AI Agent Service and went GA in May 2025. In March 2026 it was renamed 'classic' and deprecated, through a notice added to the docs without a formal announcement. It retires March 31, 2027. Its threads-and-runs design was modelled on OpenAI's Assistants API, which died in August 2026. Migrating means rebuilding on the Responses API.

Killed by: Microsoft Shutdown: March 31, 2027 Replacement: New Foundry Agent Service (Responses API: threads become conversations, runs become responses) Source
