Frequently asked questions

What is Killed by AI?

Killed by AI is an independent tracker of discontinued AI products, models, startups, and hardware. It currently documents 173 casualties of the artificial intelligence industry, including deprecated API models and billion-dollar startup failures.

Why did OpenAI shut down Sora?

OpenAI shut down the Sora app on April 26, 2026: it cost about $1M a day in compute against fewer than 500K users, and a $1B Disney deal had collapsed. The API followed on September 24, 2026. Full timeline: Sora AI shutdown.

Is ChatGPT dead?

No. ChatGPT itself is alive, but plenty of what shipped inside it is not: ChatGPT Plugins, the GPT Store, GPT-4o, the original GPT-4, the DALL·E GPT. Browse every OpenAI death on the OpenAI page.

Which company has killed the most AI products?

OpenAI leads with 31 discontinued products, followed by Google with 28.

What was the shortest-lived AI product?

Google Earth AI Image Generation lasted just 1 day. On July 30, 2026 Google put Nano Banana 2 inside Google Earth on the web: zoom to any spot on the planet, click 'create image', and describe what should be there instead. A BBC journalist and other critics pointed out that one of the most trusted sources of visual evidence for journalists and researchers now had a fake-imagery button. By the next day Google said it had seen people 'sharing screenshots of generated imagery that appear to violate our policies' and rolled the feature back while it worked on stronger guardrails. It lived about a day.
