Frequently Asked Questions

What is Killed by AI?

Killed by AI is an open-source tracker of discontinued AI products, models, startups, and hardware. It currently documents 122 casualties of the artificial intelligence industry, from deprecated API models to billion-dollar startup failures.

Why did OpenAI shut down Sora?

OpenAI shut down the Sora app on April 26, 2026 — about $1M a day in compute against fewer than 500K users, and a collapsed $1B Disney deal. The API follows on September 24. Full timeline: Sora AI shutdown.

Is ChatGPT dead?

No — ChatGPT itself is alive. But plenty of what shipped inside it is not: ChatGPT Plugins, the GPT Store, GPT-4o, the original GPT-4, the DALL·E GPT. Browse every OpenAI death on the OpenAI page.

Which company has killed the most AI products?

OpenAI leads with 27 discontinued products, followed by Google with 20.
