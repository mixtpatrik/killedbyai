Frequently Asked Questions

What is Killed by AI?

Killed by AI is an open-source tracker of discontinued AI products, models, startups, and hardware. It currently documents 91 casualties of the artificial intelligence industry, from deprecated API models to billion-dollar startup failures.

Why did OpenAI shut down Sora?

OpenAI's text-to-video model and standalone app. The Sora web and app were shut down on April 26, 2026 (API following on September 24). Peaked at ~1M users before collapsing to under 500K while burning ~$1M/day. OpenAI is freeing up compute for its coding and enterprise products, and the $1B Disney deal collapsed.

Which company has killed the most AI products?

OpenAI leads with 23 discontinued products, followed by Google with 15.

What was the shortest-lived AI product?

Microsoft Tay lasted just 1d. Microsoft's teen-persona AI chatbot on Twitter. Within 16 hours of launch, coordinated trolls taught it to tweet racist slurs, Nazi propaganda, and hate speech. Microsoft pulled the plug after just 96,000 tweets — one of AI's most infamous disasters.

What AI products were most recently discontinued?

The most recent casualties include: OpenAI Assistants API (Beta), Claude Sonnet 4 & Opus 4, Stainless SDK Generator, xAI Grok Legacy Models, DALL·E 3 API Snapshots.

How many AI products have been shut down?

We track 91 discontinued AI products across 33 deprecated models, 15 killed apps, 22 ended services, 16 failed startups, and 5 dead hardware products.

What was the OpenAI AI Text Classifier and why was it discontinued?

OpenAI's AI Text Classifier was a tool launched in January 2023 to detect AI-generated text. It was pulled in July 2023 after just 6 months because it could only correctly identify 26% of AI-written text while falsely flagging 9% of human writing.
