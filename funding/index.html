Inflection AI / Pi

$1,500M

A 'personal AI' focused on emotional intelligence and empathy, backed by $1.5B in funding. Effectively killed when Microsoft hired CEO Mustafa Suleyman and most of the engineering team, leaving Pi as an orphaned product.

Olive AI

$902M

A healthcare AI startup once valued at $4 billion that promised to automate hospital admin tasks. Despite raising $902M, investigations revealed its 'AI' relied heavily on manual human labor behind the scenes. After mass layoffs, it shut down and sold its assets for a fraction of its valuation.

startup 2017–2023 Source

Builder.ai

$445M

Microsoft-backed 'AI app builder' valued at $1.5B. Its 'neural network' turned out to be 700 engineers in India writing code manually. After inflating revenue 300% ($55M real vs $220M claimed), a creditor seized $37M from its accounts, triggering bankruptcy across five countries.

startup 2016–2025 Source

Adept AI (Independent)

$415M

An AI startup building autonomous agents that could use software tools, valued at $1B with $415M raised. Amazon hired 80% of the team including the CEO and co-founders — a 'reverse acqui-hire' that drew FTC scrutiny as a pattern of Big Tech absorbing AI startups without triggering antitrust review.

startup 2022–2024 Source

Humane AI Pin

$230M

The $699 screenless AI wearable that was supposed to replace smartphones. Plagued by overheating, terrible battery life, and painfully slow responses. Humane sought a buyer after disastrous reviews and abysmal sales.

hardware 2024–2025 Source

Character.AI (Independent)

$150M

The viral AI chatbot platform where users created and talked to AI 'characters.' After safety controversies and intense competition, Google effectively acqui-hired the founders and key staff, leaving the company a shell of its former self.

startup 2022–2024 Source

Jasper AI (Original Platform)

$125M

One of the first AI writing unicorns, valued at $1.5B. Jasper's 'wrapper' business model collapsed as GPT-4o and Claude made its core features available for free or at a fraction of the cost.

startup 2021–2025 Source

Woebot

$114M

A pioneering AI therapy chatbot used by 1.5 million people that delivered cognitive behavioral therapy via smartphone. Despite FDA Breakthrough Device designation, the company shut down because the regulatory process for AI mental health tools proved too costly and slow as LLMs outpaced the FDA's ability to regulate them.

app 2017–2025 Source

Stability AI (Stable Diffusion Creator)

$101M

The company behind Stable Diffusion, the open-source model that democratized AI image generation. After CEO Emad Mostaque resigned amid financial turmoil and copyright lawsuits, the company struggled to survive as a going concern.

startup 2020–2024 Source

Writer (GPT Wrapper Era)

$100M

Originally an AI writing assistant for enterprises built on GPT. Pivoted multiple times as the 'wrapper' model died, eventually trying to become a full AI platform. Struggled as enterprises adopted native Copilot and Gemini integrations.

startup 2020–2025 Source

Neeva

$78M

An ad-free, AI-powered search engine founded by ex-Google executives. Despite raising $77.5M, it couldn't compete with free alternatives. Acquired by Snowflake for its AI talent, and the search engine was shut down.

startup 2019–2023 Source

Moxie Robot

$54M

An $800 AI social robot designed to help children develop emotional skills, particularly popular with autistic kids. When funding collapsed, the company shut down abruptly — bricking all devices and leaving heartbroken children saying goodbye to their companion on camera. No refunds were offered.

hardware 2020–2024 Source

Robin AI

$44M

UK legal tech startup that raised $44M to build AI-powered contract review tools used by PwC and KPMG. After failing to close a $50M Series C, the company cut a third of its staff and was listed on an insolvency marketplace — just 9 months after making the Sunday Times 100 Tech list.

startup 2019–2025 Source

Yupp.ai

$33M

A crowdsourced AI-evaluation platform that paid users to compare model outputs, backed by $33M from a16z crypto. It reached 1.3 million users and still died inside a year. The labs had moved on to expert-sourced data and agentic evaluation — broad consumer preference signal simply stopped being the scarce input anyone would pay for. Co-founders Pankaj Gupta and Gilad Mishne kept it accessible for two weeks after the announcement, then closed signups permanently.

startup 2025–2026 Source

Kintsugi AI

$30M

An AI startup that spent 7 years and $30M developing voice biomarker technology to detect depression and anxiety from how people speak. Shut down after nearly 4 years waiting for FDA De Novo clearance that never came. Open-sourced all code and research before closing — a rare act of scientific integrity in an industry of quiet deaths.

startup 2019–2026 Source

Rabbit R1

$30M

A $199 'AI-native' gadget designed to replace apps with a 'Large Action Model.' Widely panned as a repackaged Android app with a $199 price tag. The hardware became essentially abandoned after the company pivoted to software-only.

hardware 2024–2025 Source

Tune AI

$20M

Backed by Accel and Flipkart, this GenAI platform offered LLM fine-tuning tools via Tune Chat and Tune Studio. Killed when major cloud providers released identical tooling at lower cost, and most users were free developers who never converted to paying customers.

startup 2018–2025 Source

Reforged Labs

$4M

Built AI-generated video ad creative for mobile game studios and won real customers — Supercell, King, Ubisoft, Playtika, Scopely — with 17 staff across Vietnam and the US. CEO Robert Huynh shut it down after three years because foundation models got good enough for studios to build the same tooling in-house: 'the gap we were selling into is closing.' Its biggest customers signed six-figure contracts; there were simply too few studios at that scale to sustain a company.

startup 2023–2026 Source

Stainless SDK Generator

$4M

The startup that auto-generated production-ready API SDKs in Python, TypeScript, Go, Java, and Kotlin. Used by OpenAI, Google, Cloudflare, Replicate, Runway, and Meta. Anthropic acquired it for $300M+ and immediately announced it would wind down all hosted products — a strategic kill to deny competitors access to critical infrastructure.

startup 2022–2026 Source

Coqui AI

$3M

An open-source text-to-speech startup that built the popular Coqui TTS library. Despite being a go-to for developers wanting voice synthesis, the company ran out of funding and shut down, releasing its final models to the community before going dark.

startup 2021–2024 Source

Yara AI

$2M

UK-based AI mental health companion offering CBT-style exercises and conversational support. The founder voluntarily shut it down after deciding AI chatbots aren't safe enough for people in real mental health crises — a rare case of ethical self-termination.

startup 2024–2025 Source

Cydoc

$0M

A bootstrapped health AI startup that operated for 7 years trying to build clinical AI tools. The founder published a detailed postmortem explaining why even technically sound health AI products die: the regulatory maze, slow hospital sales cycles, and the impossibility of competing with Big Tech's free AI offerings.
