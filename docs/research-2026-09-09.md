## 5. Notable patterns

Cloud resellers are now the slowest part of the funeral: Claude 3 Haiku got three retirement dates across Anthropic (April 20), Google (August 23) and Amazon (September 10), and Azure keeps Sora 2 alive three weeks past OpenAI's own cutoff. Meanwhile first-party preview models are dying younger than ever — Gemini Omni Flash (92 days), MAI-Image-2e (128 days), Robotics-ER 1.6 (139 days) — with successors typically announced and the old model's death date set in the same changelog entry. The other thread is labour: Mechanical Turk, Flowise and the Rapid7/ServiceNow cuts all sit under the same headline, human micro-work and low-code tooling displaced by models and coding agents, even when the companies themselves phrase it as "efficiency."
