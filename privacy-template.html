Analytics and cookies

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are read. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser (_ga and one starting with _ga_, kept for up to two years) and receives the pages you view, the site you came from, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address.

It also receives a few usage events, none of which carry your name or email address:

  • Job test: the occupation you picked, your three answers, your score, and whether you shared or retook the test.
  • Embed gallery: which chart or widget you copied, downloaded or shared.
  • Newsletter: that a signup happened and on which page, without the address.

The site does not show a cookie banner. If you do not want these cookies you can block them in your browser, use a content blocker, or install Google's opt-out add-on. The site works the same without them.

There are no advertising cookies and no other trackers. The widgets other sites embed from here set no cookies and run no analytics.

Stored on your own device

A few preferences are saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them. They are never sent anywhere.

  • Light or dark theme.
  • Cards or list view, and whether the statistics panel is open, on the homepage.
  • The chart theme you last picked in the embed gallery.
  • Whether you closed the newsletter prompt or signed up, so it is not shown to you again.
  • The last few results you got in the job test, so a shared link can tell whether a result is yours.

The job test is scored in your browser and no account is involved. Apart from the analytics event described above, a result only leaves your device if you share its link.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the IP address of each visitor and may keep it in logs for security. Killed by AI has no access to those logs.

Who receives data

These companies may process data outside the European Union, including in the United States, under their own data-transfer terms. Data is not sold, rented or passed to advertisers. A sponsored link, where the site carries one, is a plain link: the sponsor receives nothing about you unless you choose to visit it.
