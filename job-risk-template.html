#OccupationScore

How the score works

55% is your occupation. We take the AI applicability score Microsoft Research computed for {{N_OCC}} US occupations from 200,000 anonymized Bing Copilot conversations — how often AI is used for each work activity, how successfully, and how much of the job it covers — and convert it to a percentile: how many occupations are less exposed than yours. The authors stress that applicability is not displacement; a job is more than its task list.

45% is how you work. Three questions shift the score up or down using what has predicted real losses so far: early-career workers in exposed jobs are being hired less (Stanford), roles built on codified, rule-following knowledge have shrunk while tacit, experience-based ones have not, physical and on-site work sits largely outside what chatbots can do, and licences and legal accountability slow automation down.
