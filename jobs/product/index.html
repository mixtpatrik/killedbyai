Product management: jobs replaced by AI

8 layoffs where the company blamed AI named product management among the roles cut. Together those layoffs cut 15,638 jobs. That is the company-wide total, not only product management roles, so figures for different roles overlap. The largest was Meta (8,000 jobs, May 2026); the first was Dropbox in Oct 2024 and the latest HubSpot in Oct 2026. 7 of the 8 came in 2026.
