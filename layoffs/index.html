#1 Oracle

21,000

Oracle's FY2026 10-K reports about 141,000 full-time employees at May 31, 2026, down from about 162,000 a year earlier, a net drop of about 21,000 (13%), after a restructuring plan of up to $2.1B meant to improve efficiency 'including through the adoption and integration of AI technologies across certain functions.' The filing says 'the adoption and deployment of AI technologies across our operations have resulted, and may continue to result, in reductions to our workforce.' Oracle disclosed no gross layoff count.

What counts as an AI layoff here

An entry is added only when the company, its CEO, or its regulatory filing explicitly attributes the cut to AI, automation or an AI strategy. A layoff that merely happened at an AI company, or was reported alongside AI news, does not qualify. Where only a WARN filing is public, that smaller number is used and the entry says the real total is larger. Reversals are recorded too — Klarna's 700 replaced agents are here, and so is Klarna's retreat. The full rules are on the methodology page.
