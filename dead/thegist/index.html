An Israeli startup that scanned Slack, email, CRM and analytics to hand each employee a personalised summary of what mattered. It was founded in 2022 by alumni of Wix, Blizzard, OpenWeb and Gong, with a $7M pre-seed co-led by StageOne and Aleph. A pivot toward sales tooling never caught, and rather than spend the remainder the founders closed the company and said they would return the money to investors. Closure announced September 3, 2025 (Calcalist/CTech); no exact shutdown date was published.

Collateral damage: Returning capital instead of burning it is rare: the founders said investors would get their money back.
