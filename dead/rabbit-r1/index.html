Is the Rabbit R1 discontinued?

Rabbit R1

The $199 orange 'AI-native' gadget that promised a 'Large Action Model' to replace apps, and was widely panned at launch as a repackaged Android app. Rabbit kept shipping software for it, rabbitOS 2 in September 2025 and OS3 in September 2026, but OS3 no longer needs the device. On September 22, 2026 Wired reported that 'Rabbit is no longer manufacturing the R1, and the existing stock is dwindling', and that CEO Jesse Lyu has no plans for an R2. The R1 still works with OS3 and Rabbit still sells what is left.

Timeline

  1. January 9, 2024

    Rabbit introduces the R1 at CES for $199 with no subscription. The company says the first 10,000 units sold out within 24 hours.

  2. April 23, 2024

    The first batch reaches buyers in the United States, and Rabbit holds a launch event at the TWA Hotel in New York. Early reviews describe the device as unfinished.

  3. August 13, 2024

    Sales open in more EU markets and the UK. Rabbit says all new orders now ship within three business days.

  4. September 8, 2025

    Rabbit releases rabbitOS 2, which it calls its largest software update for the R1 so far.

  5. January 29, 2026

    Rabbit announces Project Cyberdeck, a small Linux computer for AI-assisted coding, as its second hardware project.

  6. September 22, 2026

    Rabbit makes OS3 generally available. It is an AI agent that runs in the cloud and works without the R1. Wired's report on the release says Rabbit is no longer manufacturing the R1 and that the existing stock is dwindling.

What replaced Rabbit R1

Rabbit moved to software that does not need the device: OS3, an AI agent that runs in the cloud and on Windows, Mac and Linux computers, and also works with the R1. Its next hardware is Project Cyberdeck, and Wired reports that CEO Jesse Lyu has no plans for an R2.

Aftermath

The R1 was never switched off. As of November 2026 Rabbit's own site still sells it for $199 with no subscription, and owners can run OS3 on it or go back to the earlier assistant in retro mode. Wired's September 2026 report says a handful of employees had gone on strike, claiming they were not being paid, and that Lyu said there were 'no issues with the company's health.' Lyu also claimed more than 100,000 R1s had been delivered.

Questions about Rabbit R1

Can you still buy a Rabbit R1?

Yes, for now. As of November 2026 Rabbit's own store lists the R1 at $199 and says it ships in three business days. Wired reported in September 2026 that Rabbit has stopped manufacturing it and that stock is dwindling, so sales last only while units remain.

Is the Rabbit R1 discontinued?

Production has ended, according to Wired's September 2026 report, and CEO Jesse Lyu said there are no plans for an R2. Rabbit has not shut the device down: it still sells the remaining units and still supports the R1 in software.

Does the Rabbit R1 still work?

Yes. The R1 works with OS3, the agent software Rabbit released in September 2026, and a retro mode brings back the earlier assistant. Rabbit says no subscription is required.

What is the R1 service portal?

Rabbit has no page with that name. Returns and warranty claims start with an email to Rabbit support, which issues a return authorization. Device settings are now in the OS3 web app; before OS3, owners managed connected services in a web portal Rabbit called the rabbithole.

Where can I find a used Rabbit R1 for sale?

Rabbit's support pages say used units appear on secondhand marketplaces such as eBay and Facebook Marketplace. Ask the seller to factory reset the R1 and unlink it from their account, because only the previous owner can unlink it. Rabbit says it cannot guarantee a used device.
